ABOUT SIGRØN

About Sigrøn

I started Sigrøn to explore opportunities to contribute to local projects, develop new ideas and collaborate with others working in environmental research and geospatial technology.

The name Sigrøn was inspired by the idea of evergreen in Old Norse, with a little play on the words “see green”. Keeping the natural environment at the forefront of my work is important to me.

My background is in human geography, and I hold a master's degree in GIS and Remote Sensing. I particularly enjoy working with geographic data, combining different datasets and developing workflows to tackle the unique challenges of individual projects.

I'm especially enthusiastic about Earth observation and the Copernicus programme. With satellite and drone technology evolving so quickly, there are exciting opportunities to explore how we observe, understand and respond to changes in our environment.

I'm continuing to develop my skills in programming, automation and GeoAI, with the aim of creating practical geospatial tools and applications.

Through Sigrøn, I hope to build connections, share ideas and contribute to meaningful projects in Norway.
